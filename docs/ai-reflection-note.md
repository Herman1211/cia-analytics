# AI Reflection Note

Mughaputo Achumi | CIA 1 | mCaffeine and FESH shilo

## What I used AI for

I used ChatGPT to help build FESH shilo, a simple website demo based on mCaffeine, and to organise the KPI audit. AI helped with the code, product layout, cart and demo checkout. It also helped map the buying journey and suggest KPIs. The demo website is separate from the real mCaffeine store.

## Prompts and AI outputs

These are shortened summaries of the instructions I gave during the project.

Prompt: Make a simple demo with just three body washes. Add the images, prices, descriptions and a working cart. Output: AI created the product collection, details view and cart.

Prompt: Use a few design elements from my NagaHub website. Make it easy on desktop and mobile. Output: AI used a simple layout with rounded cards and adjusted it for phone screens.

Prompt: Change the website name to FESH shilo and host it inside GitHub. Output: AI updated the name and saved the website in cia-analytics for GitHub Pages.

Prompt: Reconstruct the brand’s likely data funnel and evaluate what should be measured. Output: AI suggested a funnel and proposed KPIs for cart activity, checkout, order value and repeat purchases.

## My own choices and corrections

I chose mCaffeine and kept the demo to three body washes so the project stayed simple. I asked for a working customer journey, requested a design similar to my NagaHub work and changed the name to FESH shilo. I also asked AI to check my friend’s example carefully instead of accepting everything in it.

When publishing did not work, I sent screenshots. They showed that no workflow had started, even though AI had described the site as still publishing. A new commit then triggered the deployment. This showed me why I need to check the result instead of relying only on the AI’s message.

## Limits and what still needs evidence

No signed-in GA4 Demo Account report or Meta Business Suite insight was analysed. The deck uses public website observations and official tool guidance. Google’s demo data belongs to its demo properties, not mCaffeine. The proposed events and KPIs still need account checks; the simulated checkout is not real sales data.

Before final submission, the remaining evidence is a dated GA4 demo report and authorised Meta insights, clearly labelled. AI helped prepare the work, but I still need to check its claims and explain the choices in my own words.
